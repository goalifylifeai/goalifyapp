Delete your account

How to delete your Goalify account and the data stored with it.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Goalify and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email privacy@goalify.life from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Goalify account”. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details)
  • Your profile: display name, pronouns, life areas and coaching preferences
  • Goals, subtasks, habits, streaks, XP and achievements
  • Journal entries, daily intentions, ritual check-ins and your future-self letter
  • AI Coach messages and insights, and generated vision images
  • Your Circle memberships and the status other members could see

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep records of purchases. If you opted in to product analytics, the usage events linked to your account are deleted as well.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Goalify Beyond subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings to stop billing.

Questions? Email privacy@goalify.life. See also our Privacy Policy.
