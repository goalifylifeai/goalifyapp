Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 16, 2026

Goalify is a personal growth app that helps you set goals, build habits and reflect on your life. This policy explains what information Goalify collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Goalify apps for Android and iOS and to the web version at goalify.life.

1. Who is responsible

Goalify is operated by Pulkit Chaturvedi, Bartningallee 24, 10557 Berlin, Germany ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data. For anything privacy-related, email privacy@goalify.life.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash; we never see it in plain text.
  • Google or Apple sign-in. If you sign in this way, we receive your email address and an account identifier from that provider, and nothing else from your Google or Apple account.

Profile information you give us

  • Your display name, the life areas you focus on, your preferred coaching tone and your time zone.
  • Your pronouns, if you choose to give them. They are optional and only used to personalise coaching language and vision images.

Content you create

  • Goals and their steps, habits, completions, streaks, rest days and XP.
  • Daily intentions and morning and evening check-ins.
  • Journal entries. The app estimates a simple mood score for each entry on your device, and stores it with the entry.
  • Messages you send to the AI Coach and the coach's replies and insights.
  • Your letter to your future self.
  • Vision images generated for your goals.

Your journal is private. Journal entries and their mood scores are stored only so you can see them in the app, for example your mood trend. They are never sent to our AI provider, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising. You can delete any entry at any time.

Circles

If you create or join a Circle, its other members can see the display name you had when you joined, whether you completed today's main action, and your current streak. They never see your goals, habits, journal or coach conversations. We store the Circle's name, its invite code and who its members are.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Goalify Beyond, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your card or bank details. We receive and store your subscription status (for example, which plan you are on and when it renews) so we can unlock the features you paid for.

Device features

  • Calendar. If you add a habit to your calendar, the app asks for calendar permission, lists your calendars on your device so you can pick one, and creates a recurring event. We do not read your existing events. We store only the ID of the event we created, so we can update or remove it.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. If you set a reminder time for a habit, that time is saved with the habit.

Product analytics (only if you agree)

The app asks whether you want to share usage analytics. Nothing is sent unless you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time in Profile. If you agree, we collect:

  • events about how you use the app, such as screens opened, features used and actions completed;
  • basic technical details: app version, operating system and device model;
  • a random identifier for your installation, linked to your Goalify account ID once you sign in;
  • subscription events (such as starting or renewing a subscription).

We never send the text of your goals, habits, journal entries or coach messages to analytics, and location lookup is turned off.

Website

Our website and the web version of Goalify use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

4. AI features

  • AI Coach. When you open the coach or send it a message, we send your message together with your goals, habits and recent completions to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Your journal entries and mood scores are never sent. We use Google's paid API service, under whose terms Google does not use this content to train its models.
  • Vision images. Images are generated by fal.ai from a general scene description based on the goal's life area. No goal titles, journal text or other text you wrote is sent. If you turned on gender-aware coaching, the description may include a figure matching your pronouns.

If the mood scores of your recent journal entries trend lower, the app may show you a gentle check-in reminder. Our servers decide this from the scores alone, without AI, and the reminder text is the same for everyone.

AI output can be wrong. It is not medical, financial or professional advice.

Some providers above process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised by EU law, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and content for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, your account, content and analytics data are deleted. Copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as for purchases, are kept for the period the law requires.
  • If you withdraw analytics consent, no further analytics data is collected.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to some processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before.

You can edit or delete your content and export a copy of your data from the app. To delete your account, go to Profile → Delete account, or follow the steps at goalify.life/delete-account. For any other request, email privacy@goalify.life. We reply within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the one where you live or work. Ours is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and your data is encrypted at rest by our database provider. Each account can only access its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

10. Age requirement

Goalify is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email privacy@goalify.life and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
